ISSEI—EN

ISSEI—EN® is a wholesale distributor of high-grade matcha and Japanese tea. We source exclusively from Japan, partnering only with producers whose standards favor control and consistency over scale.

We believe our role is simply to be the curator. We don't embellish; we allow the qualities of the season, region, and cultivar to speak for themselves. Because our sourcing is direct and our category is narrow by design, every product is evaluated against our own standards, and whats selected is both comprehensive and deliberate.
